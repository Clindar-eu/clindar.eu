Expert support when software isn't enough

Clindar combines modern validation software with specialist clinical-data expertise for the parts of submission readiness that still require human judgement.

  • Submission readiness & CDISC delivery

    SDTM, ADaM, Define-XML, and submission outputs prepared, reviewed, and validated for FDA and EMA submission. From study data transformation through final submission-readiness review, we provide focused CDISC expertise where internal teams need additional capacity or specialist support.

  • CDISC audit & remediation

    Independent review, investigation, and remediation of SDTM, ADaM, and Define-XML before submission, inspection, or transfer between vendors. We identify conformance issues, investigate their causes, and help teams resolve validation findings efficiently — particularly where inherited datasets or vendor transitions require specialist review.

  • Submission programming & automation

    Targeted SAS, R, and Python expertise for CDISC transformations, validation workflows, submission outputs, and repeatable clinical data automation — including dataset transformations, validation pipelines, data review utilities, and TLF production.
