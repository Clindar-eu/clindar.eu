# Clindar

**Clinical data, submission-ready.**

Clindar builds tools and services for clinical data teams at CROs and small biotech companies — helping them move from raw trial data to clean, compliant, submission-ready datasets without the complexity of enterprise-priced software.

---

## What we build

### 🔍 CDISC Validator *(in development)*
A web-based CDISC conformance validator for SDTM and ADaM datasets. Built to solve the real frustrations of day-to-day clinical data work:

- **Always-current rules** — conformance rules and CDISC Controlled Terminology update automatically, no manual downloads
- **In-browser data viewer** — inspect the exact offending rows directly in the app, no SAS session required
- **Plain-English fix suggestions** — each error comes with a clear explanation and actionable fix, not just a rule reference
- **Submission readiness score** — a clear quality metric per study, suitable for sponsor reporting
- **Team workspace** — shared issue tracking for CDM, programming, and sponsor teams in one place
- **GDPR-compliant by design** — EU-hosted, raw data deleted within 24 hours of validation

> Built for the teams who can't justify enterprise pricing but need enterprise-quality compliance checks.

---

### 🗺️ SDTM Mapper *(planned)*
Map raw EDC data to SDTM domains with a visual mapping editor and reusable rule templates. Supports Medidata Rave, Veeva Vault EDC, InForm, and ODM-XML exports.

---

### 📊 TLF Generator *(planned)*
Automated tables, listings, and figures from ADaM datasets. R-based output (gt, flextable, rtables) with sponsor-defined shells and submission-ready formatting.

---

### ✅ Edit Check Builder *(planned)*
A GUI for authoring, testing, and exporting data validation rules — compatible with Rave, InForm, and ODM standards. No SAS programming required to build and maintain your edit check library.

---

### 📄 Define-XML Generator *(planned)*
Point-and-click creation of submission-ready Define-XML v2.1 from your SDTM and ADaM metadata. Validated against CDISC and FDA technical conformance rules before export.

---

### 🤖 AI-assisted SAP Reviewer *(planned)*
Automatically flag inconsistencies between a Statistical Analysis Plan, ADaM dataset specifications, and TLF shells — reducing manual QC time and catching discrepancies before programming begins.

---

### 📋 CRF Design Assistant *(planned)*
An AI-guided eCRF builder with CDASH alignment checks and edit check suggestions — helping data managers design compliant, analysis-ready case report forms from the start.

---

### 📝 Reviewer's Guide Assistant *(planned)*
AI-assisted drafting of Study Data Reviewer's Guides (SDRG) and Analysis Data Reviewer's Guides (ADRG), linked directly to your SDTM and ADaM metadata for accurate, consistent content.

---

### 📈 Automated Data Review Dashboard *(planned)*
Live data quality metrics, enrollment tracking, and medical coding QC for ongoing clinical trials — giving data managers and sponsors a real-time view of study data health.

## Who we are

Clindar is built by a clinical biometrician with 15+ years of hands-on experience across statistical programming, clinical data management, and CDISC standards delivery for CROs and pharma sponsors globally.

Clindar exists because the tools available to small clinical data teams have never matched the complexity of the work they're asked to do — and enterprise-priced platforms were never designed with them in mind.

## Who we work with

- **Small and mid-size CROs** needing scalable CDISC compliance tooling without enterprise contracts
- **Small biotech companies** preparing their first FDA or EMA submission without a full in-house biometrics function
